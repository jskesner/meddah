# 0112. Universal West Marches Campaign Paradigm (Any Play Mode) & Persistent Guild Server Architecture (`backend/app/multiplayer/guild_server/` — Phase 14 [Post-MVP])

- **Date**: 2026-11-23
- **Status**: Accepted
- **Deciders**: Maintainers, Architecture Design Session
- **Related Issues / Decisions**: Grill-Me Resolution #62 (`docs/design/technical_specification_and_plan.md` §2.1)

## Context & Problem Statement
Operational refinement and scope resolution for **Universal West Marches Campaign Paradigm (Any Play Mode) & Persistent Guild Server Architecture (`backend/app/multiplayer/guild_server/` — Phase 14 [Post-MVP])**.

## Decision
- **Scale-Elastic & Multi-Scale Hub Topology (`Scale 0` through `Scale 4`) & Nested Hub Networks (`HubTopologyBlock`)**: Expands the traditional "frontier town hub" across all 5 spatial scales: *Scale 4/3 District & Station Hubs* (safe megacity blocks, underground hacker speakeasies, orbital stations, citadel wards), *Scale 2 Regional & System Hubs* (demilitarized valleys, civilized solar systems/asteroid belts), *Scale 1 Continental & Core World Hubs* (planetary ecumenopolises, civilized core star sectors), and *Scale 0 Persistent Hub Timelines, Multiverse Universes & Pan-Dimensional Nexus Citadels* (immutable Prime Timelines, End-of-Time sanctuaries, and dimensional crossroads cities with emergency *Chronal/Dimensional Tether Retrieval*), plus *Hierarchical "Hub-and-Spoke" Nested Hub Networks* with shared or local merchandise unlock registries and scale-aware cartography/travel watch engines.
   - **Scale-Elastic & Multi-Scale Hub Topology (`Scale 0` through `Scale 4`) & Nested Hub Networks (`HubTopologyBlock`)**: Expands the traditional "frontier town hub" across all 5 spatial scales: *Scale 4/3 District & Station Hubs* (safe megacity blocks, underground hacker speakeasies, orbital stations, citadel wards), *Scale 2 Regional & System Hubs* (demilitarized valleys, civilized solar systems/asteroid belts), *Scale 1 Continental & Core World Hubs* (planetary ecumenopolises, civilized core star sectors), and *Scale 0 Persistent Hub Timelines, Multiverse Universes & Pan-Dimensional Nexus Citadels* (immutable Prime Timelines, End-of-Time sanctuaries, and dimensional crossroads cities with emergency *Chronal/Dimensional Tether Retrieval*), plus *Hierarchical "Hub-and-Spoke" Nested Hub Networks* with shared or local merchandise unlock registries and scale-aware cartography/travel watch engines.
   - **Orthogonal Campaign Paradigm vs. Guild Server Execution Mode**: The **West Marches Campaign Paradigm** (`west_marches_hubs` module) can be applied to **any play mode or campaign** (Solo Play with a character/companion roster, GMless Co-Op, Private Host-Authoritative Multiplayer on Desktop/Web, or Persistent Guild Server). Because **West Marches is the only campaign paradigm currently planned for Persistent Multi-Player Guild Server Mode (`meddah serve --mode guild-server`)**, it includes dedicated multi-player server optimizations (Atomic Expedition Settlement over CoW child instances, `treasure_bundles` + `UnlockedMerchandiseRegistry`, 4-tier item scoping, client-scoped hub quest overlays, non-aggression & `bind_on_pickup` economy guards, and deterministic disconnect hireling substitution) that are enforced in `guild-server` mode and individually optional in solo/private campaigns. All downtime services, currencies, travel costs, and conditions are strictly ruleset-defined.
   - **Hybrid Expeditions, Multi-Stage Checkpoints & Frontier Scouting**: Supports targeted Bounty Contracts (bounded instances) and open-ended Frontier Scouting Hex-Crawls (Travel-Watch survival loop, push-your-luck danger scaling, community cartography reveals, and unmapped POI stub discovery); multi-stage quests track Stage Checkpoints in personal journals for dynamic roster substitution; and hub-based urban quests use client-scoped `QuestStateEvaluator` overlays.
   - **"Treasure Bundles & Merchant Unlock" Loot Mode (`loot_mode: "treasure_bundles"`)**: No in-dungeon persistent gear drops; performance-based treasure bundles convert to ruleset primary currency/wealth tokens and campaign guild renown marks at Atomic Settlement, while discovered items write to the `UnlockedMerchandiseRegistry` enabling all completing players to purchase/commission them at Hub Quartermasters (preventing ninja-looting and grinding).
   - **Hub Services, Deep Persistent Afflictions & Infinite/Tiered Merchant Stock**: Downtime services spawn only facilities defined in `.rpgrules` (enchanting in fantasy, cyberware tuning in sci-fi, trauma surgery in gritty realism); severe conditions tagged `persistent_condition_flag` persist across sessions requiring specialized temple healing or player rituals; merchants feature infinite base stock with per-PC unlock tiers based on quests, faction rep, and `ProgressionTierMetric`.
   - **Safe Sanctuary & Zoned PvP**: Hubs enforce default non-aggression (theft, pickpocketing, and PvP attacks disabled); PvP is restricted strictly to designated `@spatial.pvp_enabled: true` arena/sparring nodes.
   - **Dual-Tier Housing & Mobile Vessel Retrofit**: Supports individual apartments/homes and pooled Guild Halls with permissioned storage (`Owner-Only` vs. `Shared Guild Stash`), plus dry-dock **Housing to Mobile Vessel Conversion** (`HoldingToVesselConverter` via Decision #26) transferring 100% of interior decor, rooms, and storage into a mobile forward base.
   - **Two-Tier Companion Architecture & Deterministic Disconnect Handling**: Common Guild Hirelings (public instance clones with standard tactical stances) vs. Personal Retinue (account-bound with settlement-phase deed/trust reconciliation and zero in-dungeon mutiny); Solo Expedition support with configurable NPC party allowances (`max_party_size`, default: `4`; `max_companions_per_party`, default: `3`); and configurable reconnect grace periods (`reconnect_grace_seconds`, default: `600s` / 10m) with deterministic Zero-AI NPC companion substitution.
   - **Dynamic 4-Axis Channel Creation & Multi-Guild Support**: GUI/MUD channel builder across nature (social vs. diegetic), scope, access gates, and permissions, plus prebuilt Guild Chat, Global OOC, Newbie/Help, and Hub LFG channels, supporting multiple independent guilds and factions.
   - **Zero-Trust Hardened Security Profile (`ZeroTrustServerProfile`) & 100% Admin Configurability**: All operational parameters, limits, and timeouts are fully configurable by server admins in `server.toml` or CLI flags with sensible defaults (`max_concurrent_instances: 10`, `instance_idle_timeout_seconds: 1800s`, `max_connections_per_ip: 3`, `rate_limit_per_second: 3`, `account_disk_quota_bytes: 2MB`, `report_context_lines: 50`); audio broadcasts disabled by default, SVG paper-doll portraits default, parameterized SQL queries, MUD HTML/URI XSS sanitization, AI prompt-injection bounding tags, per-IP rate/connection limits, and private moderation hierarchy (Admin/Marshal/Guide/Player, `> mute`, `> detain / jail`, `> ban`, `> report` with context capture, instance-scoped X-Card).

## Consequences

### Negative / Trade-offs
