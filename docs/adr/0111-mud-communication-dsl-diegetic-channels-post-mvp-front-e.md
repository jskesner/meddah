# 0111. MUD Communication DSL, Diegetic Channels & Post-MVP Front-End Visual FX / Graphical Randomizer Guardrails

- **Date**: 2026-11-23
- **Status**: Accepted
- **Deciders**: Maintainers, Architecture Design Session
- **Related Issues / Decisions**: Grill-Me Resolution #61 (`docs/design/technical_specification_and_plan.md` §2.1)

## Context & Problem Statement
Operational refinement and scope resolution for **MUD Communication DSL, Diegetic Channels & Post-MVP Front-End Visual FX / Graphical Randomizer Guardrails**.

## Decision
- **Multiplayer 3D Dice & Card-Draw Synchronization**: Public rolls and card draws animate identically on every opted-in player's screen using the server-broadcast `physics_throw_seed`; GM-Screen or Secret/Blind checks (*Stealth, Perception, Hole Cards*) animate face-up only for the authorized seat(s) while optionally displaying a mystery `"GM is rolling..."` shadow die or face-down card back (`[🂠]`) to other seats.
   - **Multiplayer 3D Dice & Card-Draw Synchronization**: Public rolls and card draws animate identically on every opted-in player's screen using the server-broadcast `physics_throw_seed`; GM-Screen or Secret/Blind checks (*Stealth, Perception, Hole Cards*) animate face-up only for the authorized seat(s) while optionally displaying a mystery `"GM is rolling..."` shadow die or face-down card back (`[🂠]`) to other seats.
   - **Priority Cross-Fading for Simultaneous Condition Shaders (No Slot Mixing)**: When an entity has multiple active conditions with `ConditionVisualSpec` shaders, the `PortraitCompositor` and `TokenRenderer` never mix shader slots across different conditions at the same instant—instead, they render each condition's complete, cohesive shader package and **smoothly priority-cross-fade** across active conditions (weighted by severity/priority), while **all** active conditions continuously display their mini-icon badges along the portrait bezel and token ring.
   - **Multi-Scope Named Card Decks (`DeckBlock`)**: Rulesets can define any number of named `DeckBlock` instances scoped to `table_shared` (central Fate/Action/Initiative/Tarot deck), `per_entity` (personal player/enemy modifier or ability decks), or `encounter_local`, with a Deck Selector Pill in `CardDeckVisualizer.tsx`.
   - **Linked Portrait $\leftrightarrow$ VTT Map Token Synchronization**: Framed Ring and 2.5D Standee tokens dynamically mirror the portrait's active Vitality state (`Healthy -> Bloodied -> Critical -> Dead`), paper-doll gear updates, and priority cross-faded `ConditionVisualSpec` shader by default, with an optional per-entity custom top-down/standee sprite override.
   - **Non-Interrupting `whisper` Eavesdropping**: Whispers show a non-spoiler observer emote (`"Kale leans in and whispers quietly to Vane"`) and silently check against bystanders' Passive Perception/Observation (or roll a silent background check if an entity has actively toggled `> eavesdrop @target` or has a keen-hearing trait), leaking partial/full text only on a pass without interrupting chat flow with popup roll prompts.
   - **Three-Tier Channel & Frequency Alias Registry (`ChannelPresetRegistry`)**: Supports **(1) World/Ruleset Canonical Presets** (`#party-tac`, `#local-news`), **(2) Shared Party Presets**, and **(3) Personal Comms Contacts**—allowing players to bookmark any discovered or intercepted frequency during play (`> radio save syndicate-net 88.45MHz --party`) into campaign state, autocomplete, and the Comms HUD.
   - **Diegetic Medium Physical Room Audibility (`audibility_mode`), Condition Gating (`Silence`/`Paralysis`/`Deafened`), Sealed-Helmet / Vacuum Physics & Fine-Grained PA Voice Modulation**: Each `CommunicationMediumBlock` declares `audibility_mode` (`"audible_speech" | "subvocal_whisper" | "silent_mental_or_text"`) plus input modality gates (`requires_vocal_speech`, `requires_somatic_input`, `requires_mental_action`). Vocal-blocking conditions (`#muted`, `#gagged`, `#silenced`) block `say` and voice radios while still permitting somatic text terminals, visual `sign` language, or telepathy; `#paralyzed`/`#petrified` blocks both vocal and somatic channels while still permitting pure mental/telepathic links (`requires_mental_action: true`). Wearing a **Sealed Space-Suit / Diving Helmet (`#sealed_helmet`)** isolates voice inside the helmet so suit radio transmissions have `0` external room bleed (unless the external PA speaker is toggled on). When the external PA speaker is used, any metallic/vocoder filter is **100% optional** (defaulting to `clean_natural`), and rulesets/gear with `VoiceModulatorProfile` grant **fine-grained player control** over PA projection (`whisper_amp`, `normal`, `loud_hailer`, `booming_resonance`) and vocal timbre/masking (`clean_natural`, `metallic_vocoder`, `deep_dreadnought`, `scrambled_anonymous`, or `voice_mimic:@target`). **`#vacuum`** blocks open-air sound completely while supporting suit radios, visual `sign`s, and conductive `touch-helmet @target` speech.
   - **Ruleset-Grounded Diegetic Languages, Dialects, Written Scripts & Secret Cants (`LanguageBlock`)**: Supports inline (`> say in elvish "..."`) and Comms-Bar language selection across all spoken and written channels (including radios, broadcasts, telepathy, and couriers) with per-seat server-side fluency filtering (**Fluent / Translator** $\rightarrow$ full text; **Partial Fluency / Related Dialect** $\rightarrow$ root words + masked syllables; **Non-Speaker** $\rightarrow$ deterministic phonetic cipher or non-spoiler tag, with raw plaintext stripped from the wire), plus dual-layer **Secret Cants** (*Thieves' Cant / Druidic*) and `language_dependent` vs. `universal_conceptual` telepathy modes.
   - **Semantic Social Valence Tagging (`Tier A` & `Tier B/C`)**: All 60+ social emotes and vocal `(tone)` modifiers carry structured semantic valence tags (`respectful_greeting`, `hostile_intimidation`, `affectionate_comfort`, `sarcastic_taunt`) that deterministically shift NPC disposition/templates in `Tier A` (`deterministic_dialogue_broker.py`) and feed the NPC/Companion micro-prompt, TTS prosody, and portrait expression state swaps in `Tier B/C`.
   - **Unified "Comms & Channels Pill Bar" + Filterable MUD Stream Tabs**: The MUD input bar features a quick **Channel Selector Pill** (click or `Tab`-cycle between Local `say`, `#party-tac`, `#telepathy`, `Whisper`, and `OOC`), a collapsible **Comms Tuner Widget** (signal strength, jamming status, earpiece/mute toggles, and saved presets), and filterable MUD stream tabs (`All`, `In-Character`, `Comms/Radio`, `Combat/Rolls`, `OOC`).
   - **Four-Bus Client Audio Director, Live GM Soundboard/DJ, Diegetic 1-Hop Room Acoustics, Player Audio Broadcasting & Per-Player Mixer (`Section 1.7.6`)**: (1) **Live GM Soundboard & Custom Playlist DJ** supporting local `MP3/OGG/FLAC/WAV/OPUS` + `.m3u` imports, Reactive Auto-DJ mood crossfading with TTS ducking, Manual GM DJ override (`[Dramatic Cut to Silence]`), and 1-click programmable SFX pads (`> sfx <tag>`); (2) **Diegetic In-Room Emitters & 1-Hop Door Occlusion (`SpatialAudioFilter.ts`)** applying a real-time Web Audio low-pass filter (`~350 Hz`, `-14dB`) to adjacent-room bards/jukeboxes/radios through closed doors (sweeping open when the door opens) plus room-geometry reverb (`vaulted_cathedral_echo`, `metallic_ship_corridor`) and `#sealed_helmet`/`#vacuum` attenuation; (3) **Player-Broadcast Audio Clips & Bard Performances (`PlayerAudioBroadcaster.ts`)** with capability/item-bound clips (`audio_clip_ref`), MUD commands (`> play <clip_id>`, `> act [audio: <clip>] "..."`), in-room diegetic spatialization, closed-door occlusion, `#vacuum`/`#silence` gating, Host table policy (`allow_player_audio`), automatic `-14 LUFS` loudness normalization, and per-player client volume/mute controls; and (4) **Granular Per-Player Audio Sync & Comfort Controls** with independent `Master / Music / Ambience / SFX / Voice` sliders, local selection between *Neural TTS*, *OS Speech*, *Retro MUD Text-Blip Synth*, or *Silent Subtitles*, and audio comfort toggles (no tinnitus/ringing, Night-Mode dynamic range compression).

## Consequences
### Positive
- Establishes an explicit, version-controlled architectural boundary aligned with Meddah's 4 Golden Rules.

### Negative / Trade-offs
- Requires strict adherence to schema validation, determinism, and modularity contracts during implementation.

### Neutral
- Recorded as part of the initial baseline architecture; revisions require a superseding ADR.
